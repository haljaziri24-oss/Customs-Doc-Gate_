Readings are the AI first reader's. Nothing here has been through a second reader, so every value still carries one pair of eyes only.

HS codes are compared on the first six digits, so a declaration that extends the invoice code to eight or ten digits counts as agreement. Package counts and weights are compared on their digits, so "24 pallets" and "24" agree. Company names are compared ignoring case and punctuation, so "WLL" and "W.L.L." agree.
